ANISHA BIRTHDAY WEBSITE
=======================

Folders:
- assets/images/  -> put your teddy/cake/background/other image files here
- assets/music/   -> put your music here

MUSIC:
The site looks for exactly:
assets/music/happy-birthday.mp3

You can rename your audio file to that name, or change musicPath near the top of js/app.js.

RUN:
Open index.html in a browser, or deploy the whole folder to any static host.

IMPORTANT:
The first button starts the music because mobile browsers generally block autoplay until the visitor interacts with the page.

All 30 sections are already included in js/messages.js. The supplied wording is kept as provided.
